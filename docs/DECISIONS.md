# Decisions

A running log of what has been decided, by whom, and what is still open. Read it before proposing a
change of direction; add to it when a decision is made. Newest entries go at the bottom of each list.

Three kinds of entry:

- **Fixed** — set by the owner in [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md). Do not revisit. If one seems wrong or
  impossible, stop and ask the owner; do not work around it.
- **Working** — made while building. Can change, but only with a new entry saying why.
- **Open** — needs the owner's answer, or needs checking against current sources. Each says who
  resolves it and when it starts to block work.

| W22 | 2026-11-17 | **How a take is placed.** Edit time of the first sample = its stream time + (edit time − stream time, sampled while the take plays) + one block − round trip. Sampling at stop was wrong whenever playback had stopped under the take: Tracktion stops the playhead whenever it rebuilds its device list (`DeviceManager::clearAllContextDevices`, after an input, output or MIDI device change), and a stopped playhead maps every time to where it stopped, which put takes up to 150 ms off the beat. The extra block: Tracktion's playback clock runs one block ahead of its device stream time (a synced context is given the coming block, then advanced again before rendering), so without it every take lands one buffer early. If playback stops or jumps under a take, the take ends there and is still placed in time (TakeWatch, `engine/TakePlacement.h`); right after playback starts the offset settles by one block, so a move of up to two blocks (at least 10 ms) is not a jump (a fixed 10 ms ended takes after 11 ms at 48 kHz / 512); the pickers and device settings lock while recording. Measured with `carthage-bench align` through a loopback on the iD44: with a measured round trip, every click lands 7.9–8.0 samples early (0.16–0.18 ms) at 44.1/48/96 kHz and buffers 32–1024; that remainder is constant in time, and is the difference between the two ways the bench finds a click (threshold onset vs the probe's cross-correlation). | Phase 1 exit check: "a take is recorded and plays back in time". |
